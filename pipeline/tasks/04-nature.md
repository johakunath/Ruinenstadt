# 04 Nature

Status: open

Street trees (10 m), park trees, bushes, reeds for the Spree and Müggelsee banks, sunflowers (Alexanderplatz). Upright sprites, bottom centre anchor, a single view (no sides needed, upright sprites look the same from every side in this camera). Overgrown and unsettling, not cosy. Several variants per type (at least 4 street trees) so rows of trees do not repeat; leaf and branch parts may be mirrored.

Wind: trees sway slightly in code (skew around the trunk base), so keep trunk and crown in one sprite but leave a clean trunk base. No baked shadow.
