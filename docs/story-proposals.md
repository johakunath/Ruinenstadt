# Story proposals (2026-11-11, decision D1 in BACKLOG.md)

Rough directions only. Each one reuses what the game already has: the bosses (Hauptmann von Köpenick, Spreehexe, Volkseigener Koloss, Bassprediger, Fliegerkönig, Brandmeister, Rattenmutter, Zitadellenritter, Plattenkönig), the survived GDR at Alexanderplatz, the neon and implants at Potsdamer Platz, the dinosaur jungle look of Köpenick, the U-Bahn network as progression, the camp at Warschauer Straße, Litfaßsäulen and hidden blessings, survivors and the Kiezwache. Story stays light: artefacts, tapes, plaques, one line per NPC, no dialogue trees.

## A. Der Stillstand (time)

The GDR never fell. Its last big project, the "Zeitnormal", ran Berlin's clocks from the Weltzeituhr and the Fernsehturm antenna. One night it broke, and every district slipped into a different time: Köpenick back into prehistory (jungle, raptors), Alexanderplatz frozen in 1989 and grown wild (clockwork), Potsdamer Platz flung into a neon future (implants, Kabelghule), Spandau into the Middle Ages (Zitadelle, knights), Tempelhof into 1948 (airlift ghosts, Fliegerkönig).

- The player: a Schaffner of the last U-Bahn that still runs on "Normalzeit". Every freed line pulls a district back into the present.
- Bosses: each holds one gear wheel of the Weltzeituhr. Blessings are time splinters from the Litfaßsäulen.
- Progression: district = era, so new districts are picked by era: Charlottenburg (Prussian baroque court, 1700s), Wedding (1920s industry, AEG), Wannsee (1900s villas and lakes, quiet and rural), Marzahn (Plattenbau future that never came).
- Finale: the Fernsehturm. Choice at the end: restart the clock (everything returns, also the old order) or leave the city in its times.
- Why it fits: explains why every district looks like a different game today. Strongest link to what exists.

## B. Das Myzel (nature and memory)

A fungus and root network grew out of the Spree after the collapse. It feeds on memories and rebuilds each district in the shape of what its people remembered most: Köpenick's Hauptmann legend, Xhain's squats and the Spreehexe, the GDR at Alexanderplatz, the advertising neon at Potsdamer Platz. Wurzler and Kernspeier are its first fruits.

- The player: a survivor with no memory, which is why the Myzel cannot read or control them. Each boss kill returns a memory (a tape for the Kodex).
- Bosses: memory knots grown into legends. Blessings are spores the player can tolerate.
- Progression: deeper into the Myzel = closer to the water. New districts: Wannsee and Grunewald (where the roots are oldest, lakes, rural), Wedding (Panke, flooded cellars).
- Finale: the mother root under the Tempelhofer Feld. Ending reveals who the player was.
- Why it fits: plants, rats and jungle already exist; gives the hidden blessings and tapes a reason. Darker tone.

## C. Die Kiezfürsten (satire)

The "Große Ausfall": power, network and Senat gone in one night. Every district became a small state run by a prince who plays a Berlin cliché to the extreme: the Hauptmann (fake authority with a stolen uniform), the Spreehexe (Xhain commune), the Volkseigener Koloss (GDR restoration committee), the Bassprediger (techno cult with implants), the Fliegerkönig (airlift cargo cult on the Feld), the Zitadellenritter (Spandau declares independence), the Plattenkönig.

- The player: the last BVG courier. Reconnecting the S+U network brings trade, people and stations back to the camp.
- Bosses: princes. Blessings are favours from Litfaßsäulen "Bekanntmachungen".
- Progression: line by line across the network map. New districts: Charlottenburg (West-Berlin old money, Schloss, a KaDeWe fortress), Wedding (Kiez gangs, Soldiner Straße), Wannsee (villa enclave with sailing-boat pirates).
- Finale: BER, which finally opened, as the fortress of whoever caused the Ausfall (running gag).
- Why it fits: funniest, Berlin humour, makes the U-Bahn map the centre. Lighter tone, less mystery.
